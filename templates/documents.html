How RAG Chunking Works

  1. PDF Text Extraction: The system reads all page sheets from the uploaded prospectus.
  2. Chunk Separation: Text is segmented into 800-character units with a 150-character safety overlap.
  3. Vector Representation: Gemini API's text-embedding-004 models encode chunks into 768-dimension vectors.
  4. Binary Storage: Vector arrays are packed and saved directly in SQLite as fast binary blobs.
  5. Query Retrieval: Questions from students are matches using cosine similarity against prospectus vectors to extract context.
